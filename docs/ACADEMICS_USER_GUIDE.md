## 1. Getting Started

### Selecting Your School

Before using any feature in the Academics module, you must select your school from the school selector in the top-left corner of the sidebar. All data in the Academics module is scoped to the selected school.

### Navigation

The sidebar provides quick access to all Academics features:

- **Overview** — Dashboard with key metrics and quick actions
- **Students** — Student directory, profiles, and registration
- **Attendance** — Daily attendance recording and tracking
- **Grades & Assessments** — Gradebook, grading policies, and grade recording
- **Scheduling** — Class sections, timetables, and classrooms
- **Curriculum** — Course catalog, grade levels, and standards

---

## 2. Overview Dashboard

The Overview page is your starting point. It displays:

- **Quick Stats** — Total students enrolled, active courses, class sections, and staff count
- **Action Cards** — One-click access to common tasks like adding a student, recording attendance, or creating a course

Use the action cards to quickly navigate to the feature you need.

## 3. Student Directory

### Viewing Students

Navigate to **Students** to see a searchable, filterable list of all students at your school.

- **Search** — Type a student's name or student number in the search bar
- **Filter by Grade** — Use the grade level dropdown to narrow results
- **Filter by Status** — Show only active, inactive, graduated, or withdrawn students

### Student Quick View

Click any student row to open the **Student Details drawer** on the right side. This drawer shows:

- Student photo and basic information
- Student number and enrollment status
- Quick stats: current grade level, GPA, and attendance rate
- Demographics: date of birth, gender, ethnicity, language
- Contact information: email, phone, address
- Guardian information with contact details
- Special programs and accommodations

From the drawer, you can:
- **View Full Profile** — Opens the complete student profile page
- **Edit Student** — Modify student information (three-dot menu)
- **Export** — Download student data (three-dot menu)
- **Withdraw Student** — Initiate the withdrawal process (three-dot menu, active students only)

### Student Profile Page

The full Student Profile page has five tabs:

1. **Overview** — Summary of the student's academic standing
2. **Enrollment** — Enrollment history across academic years
3. **Family** — Guardian and emergency contact details
4. **Schedule** — The student's current class schedule (sections enrolled in)
5. **Grades** — Grade history with GPA calculation

### Registering a New Student

1. Click **Add Student** on the Student Directory page
2. Complete the multi-step registration wizard:
   - **Step 1:** Basic information (name, date of birth, gender)
   - **Step 2:** Contact information and address
   - **Step 3:** Guardian information
   - **Step 4:** Medical information and special programs
   - **Step 5:** Review and submit
3. The student is automatically assigned a student number

## 4. Attendance Management

### Recording Daily Attendance

1. Navigate to **Attendance**
2. Select a **section** from the dropdown (e.g., "Algebra I - Section 001")
3. Select the **date** using the date picker (defaults to today)
4. For each student, click the appropriate status button:
   - **P** — Present (green)
   - **A** — Absent (red)
   - **L** — Late (yellow)
   - **E** — Excused (blue)
5. Optionally add a note for any student
6. Click **Submit Attendance** to save

### Quick Actions

- **Mark All Present** — Sets all students to "Present" (useful as a starting point, then mark exceptions)
- **Mark All Absent** — Sets all students to "Absent" (useful for snow days or closures)

### Daily Summary

The attendance page shows a summary dashboard with:
- Total students in the section
- Number present, absent, late, and excused
- Attendance rate percentage

### Navigating Dates

Use the left/right arrows next to the date picker to move to the previous or next day. The **Today** button returns to the current date. Future dates are disabled.

## 5. Grades & Assessments

### Gradebook

The Gradebook is where teachers record and manage student grades.

1. Navigate to **Grades & Assessments**
2. Select a **section** and **term** (e.g., Fall 2025)
3. The gradebook displays a spreadsheet-like grid with students as rows and assignments as columns
4. Click **Record Grades** to enter grades for a new assignment:
   - Enter assignment name, type (quiz, test, homework, project), and total points
   - Enter each student's earned points
   - Click **Save** to record

### Grading Policies

Grading policies define how grades are calculated. To manage policies:

1. Click the **Grading Policies** tab
2. View existing policies with their grade scales and category weights
3. Click **Create Policy** to define a new one:
   - **Grade Scale** — Define letter grade ranges (e.g., A = 90-100, B = 80-89)
   - **Category Weights** — Set weights for assignment categories (e.g., Tests = 40%, Homework = 20%, Quizzes = 20%, Participation = 10%, Projects = 10%). Weights must sum to 100%.
   - **Rounding Rule** — Choose standard, round up, or round down

### Finalizing Grades

At the end of a term:

1. Open the Gradebook for the section and term
2. Click **Finalize Grades**
3. The finalization wizard guides you through:
   - **Step 1:** Review all grades for completeness
   - **Step 2:** Confirm final grades
   - **Step 3:** Lock grades (finalized grades cannot be changed)

## 6. Scheduling

### Class Sections

Class sections are the core scheduling unit — a specific offering of a course with a teacher, time, and room.

1. Navigate to **Scheduling**
2. The **Class Sections** tab shows all sections with filters for course, teacher, and academic year
3. Click a section row to view its details:
   - Section info (course, teacher, room, capacity)
   - Enrolled students (roster)
   - Quick links to take attendance or open the gradebook

### Creating a New Section

1. Click **Add Section**
2. Fill in the section form:
   - Select a **course** from the catalog
   - Assign a **primary teacher**
   - Set **maximum enrollment** capacity
   - Select the **academic year** and optionally a **term**
   - Enter a **room number** (text field)
   - Add **co-teachers** if needed
3. Click **Create** to save

### Managing the Roster

From the Section Detail page:

1. Click the **Roster** tab
2. Click **Add Student** to enroll a student in this section
3. Search for the student by name and click to enroll
4. To remove a student, click the remove button next to their name
5. The system enforces capacity limits — you cannot exceed the maximum enrollment

### Timetables

The **Timetables** tab provides a visual weekly grid view of all scheduled sections. Filter by teacher or room to see specific schedules.

### Classrooms

The **Classrooms** tab shows all rooms in use at your school with the sections assigned to each room.

## 7. Curriculum Management

### Course Catalog

1. Navigate to **Curriculum**
2. The **Courses** tab shows all courses in your school's catalog
3. Use filters to narrow by subject area, course type, credit type, or status
4. Click a course to view its details in a drawer:
   - Course code, name, description
   - Subject area, credits, duration
   - Grade levels, prerequisites
   - Learning objectives and materials

### Creating a Course

1. Click the **more actions** menu (three dots) in the page header
2. Select **Add Course**
3. Fill in the course form:
   - **Code** and **Name** (required)
   - **Subject Area** — Mathematics, English Language Arts, Science, etc.
   - **Course Type** — Required, Elective, Honors, AP, IB, etc.
   - **Credits** and **Credit Type**
   - **Grade Levels** — Which grades can take this course
   - **Duration** — Semester, Full Year, Quarter, or Trimester
   - **Description**, **Objectives**, and **Prerequisites** (optional)
4. Click **Create Course** to save

### Editing a Course

1. Click a course in the table to open its drawer
2. Click **Edit** in the drawer
3. Modify any fields
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Grade Levels

The **Grade Levels** tab shows a table of all grade levels (Pre-K through Grade 12) with:
- Number of courses assigned to each grade level
- Course names for each grade

Click a grade level row to open a detail drawer showing all assigned courses.

## 8. Enrollment Management

### Enrollment Dashboard

Navigate to **Students** → click on the Enrollment view (accessible from the student registration flow).

The Enrollment Dashboard shows:
- **Summary Cards** — Total enrolled, by grade level, by status
- **Enrollment Table** — Full list with search and filters
- **Bulk Actions** — Withdraw or transfer students

### Enrolling a Student

Annual enrollment happens during student registration (see [Registering a New Student](#registering-a-new-student)). When you create a student, they are automatically enrolled for the current academic year.

### Withdrawing a Student

1. Find the student in the Student Directory
2. Click to open their drawer
3. Click the three-dot menu → **Withdraw Student**
4. Confirm the withdrawal in the dialog
5. The student's status changes to "Withdrawn"

### Transferring a Student

1. Open the Enrollment Dashboard
2. Find the student in the enrollment table
3. Click the transfer action
4. Select the destination school
5. Confirm the transfer

## 9. Teachers & Staff

### Staff Directory

Navigate to **Scheduling** or the **Teachers** view to see all staff at your school.

The Staff Directory shows:
- Staff name, role, email
- Employment status (active, on leave, etc.)
- Assigned sections

Click a staff member to open their detail drawer with full profile information and a list of their assigned sections.

## 10. Tips & Best Practices

### Daily Workflow

A typical daily workflow for a school administrator:

1. **Morning** — Open Attendance, select each section, record attendance
2. **During the day** — Use the Student Directory to look up student info, check schedules
3. **After school** — Open the Gradebook, record grades for completed assignments
4. **Weekly** — Review the Attendance Reports for trends, check enrollment dashboard

### Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Escape** — Close any open drawer or modal
- **Tab** — Navigate between interactive elements
- Attendance recording supports quick keyboard entry

### Data Export

Most data views support CSV export. Look for the **Export** option in page action menus or drawer dropdown menus. Exported files open in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application.

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues:
- Check your school selection in the sidebar
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact your school district's EdForge administrator
